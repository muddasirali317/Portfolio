Case study · Sindh · Pakistan

Mapping a flood as it transforms land.

A multi-temporal satellite investigation of the 2022 Sindh floods, tracing built-up signatures, vegetation response, and the evolving inundation footprint.

22 visual outputsNDBI + NDVIFlood extentApril–November
01 · Built environment

NDBI change sequence

Seven satellite outputs track the Normalized Difference Built-up Index across the study period, revealing changes in exposed and built surfaces as flood conditions developed and receded.

02 · Vegetation response

NDVI change sequence

The vegetation-index series exposes spatial stress and recovery patterns, supported by a monthly comparison between pre-flood and flood-year conditions.

03 · Inundation

Flood extent evolution

Six classified satellite views isolate the changing water footprint across Sindh and communicate the event’s spatial reach against a high-detail basemap.
