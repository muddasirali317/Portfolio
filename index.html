I'm comfortable working async, managing my own QA pipeline, and hitting accuracy/KPI targets without close supervision — and I'm currently open to remote zoning, parcel, or land-use data roles.

Selected Work,
Measured Impact.

Five case studies showing how I move from an open spatial question to a defensible method, clear evidence, and a decision-ready map.

PROJECT 01Seismic Intelligence

Awaran Earthquake — Pre-Seismic Anomaly Detection

Investigated whether satellite-observed geophysical behaviour contained meaningful signals before the Mw 7.7 Awaran earthquake. Five parameters were evaluated through statistical, machine-learning, and time-series models to separate potential precursors from ordinary variation.

OutcomeA comparative anomaly framework for identifying and communicating pre-seismic patterns across spatial maps and daily time series.

PROJECT 02Disaster Mapping

Sindh Floods 2022 — Impact & Damage Assessment

Combined Sentinel-2 imagery with NDVI, NDBI, and flood-extent mapping to trace environmental change from the pre-flood months through the 2022 event. The resulting sequence communicates shifting vegetation, built-up signatures, and inundation across Sindh.

OutcomeA multi-temporal evidence set supporting damage assessment, land-cover classification, and future flood-susceptibility modelling.

PROJECT 03Site Intelligence

Peshawar Landfill Site Suitability Study

Built a multi-criteria suitability model for responsible waste-disposal siting. Environmental and accessibility factors were weighted through AHP, normalized, and combined through weighted overlay.

OutcomeA ranked suitability surface highlighting the strongest candidate zones for further assessment.

PROJECT 04Species Distribution

Reticulitermes Species Distribution in China

Supported a Chinese postdoctoral researcher by converting documented records of R. aculabialis, R. chinensis, R. flaviceps, and R. labralis into a consistent spatial dataset and comparative distribution map.

OutcomePublication-ready research cartography revealing geographic overlap and separation among four termite species.

PROJECT 05Zoning Automation

Zoning Change Monitor — Automated Detection Across US Cities

Built a Python system that checks city zoning layers published on ArcGIS REST services and reports exactly which cities changed and what changed: boundaries, new or removed zone codes, attributes, schema, or a broken source. It filters out republishing noise such as reordered features, renumbered IDs and split polygons, and only reports "unchanged" after verifying it.

OutcomeChange polygons, priority scores and an interactive review map, so the data team re-processes only the cities that changed. Stress-tested on 500 simulated cities with every one classified correctly.
